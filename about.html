A place for small beginnings

Commit Café started with one table, one coffee pot, and a lot of ideas. There is a seat here for first-time visitors and familiar faces alike.

What we care about

  • Making newcomers feel welcome, whether they stay for five minutes or an afternoon.
  • Taking time to learn from one another.
  • Leaving things a little better than we found them.

This café is fictional. Its website is a real place to practice collaboration.
